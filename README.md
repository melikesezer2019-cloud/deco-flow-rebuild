# Growth Audit — Deco

**Live:** https://melikesezer2019-cloud.github.io/deco-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Deco - AI Home Design already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Deco's own design. Prepared by Adapty, based on the live iOS flow (Turkish storefront) and Deco's App Store and Google Play listings.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give one second chance when the paywall closes — the yearly plan alone, next to what a year of weekly costs | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Let people pick what to redesign (a room, the outside, a garden) and make the style grid a real choice | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | Name their room and their style on the paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Show their style before the price — a loader that reads choices back, then the before/after in that style | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Show the yearly plan as a weekly price (₺34,62/hafta) | ARPU +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Deco. Every price, style name, feature line and image comes from Deco's own App Store and Google Play listings and its in-app screens.

## Run locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000. Add `?open=proto` to land on the prototype.
