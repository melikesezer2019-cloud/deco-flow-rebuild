What this is

We opened Deco the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Deco: installed it, went through the first screens on the Turkish storefront, read your App Store and Google Play listings, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people.

What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — your orange pill button, your heavy headlines, your style tiles with the grey label band, the black toggle screen and the black paywall with its plan cards. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us — we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
